# TLauncher 1.0.2 is out

Unified Spotlight combines quick search with a fullscreen app library, folders, Dock pins and separate activation settings. Icons are reused across sessions; graphics resources are released when Spotlight closes. Choose your motion speed and use native frosted glass where Windows supports it.
